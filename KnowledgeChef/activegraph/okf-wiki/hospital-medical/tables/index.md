# Tables

- [appointments](appointments.md) - Appointment bookings between patients and doctors.
- [availability](availability.md) - Doctor availability slots by date and time.
- [doctors](doctors.md) - Doctors, specialties, hospital names, offices, and contact details.
- [insurance](insurance.md) - Patient insurance enrollment and coverage periods.
- [medical_records](medical_records.md) - Patient medical records authored by doctors.
- [patients](patients.md) - Patient demographics, contact information, insurance number, and clinical attributes.
- [prescriptions](prescriptions.md) - Medication prescriptions issued to patients by doctors.
- [procedure_coverage](procedure_coverage.md) - Procedure coverage rates by insurance plan.
