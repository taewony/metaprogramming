# Tables

- [customers](customers.md) - Customers with contact details, membership grade, points, and active status.
- [order_items](order_items.md) - Line items connecting orders to products with quantity and price details.
- [orders](orders.md) - Orders placed by customers with status, total amount, and order timestamp.
- [products](products.md) - Products with brand, SKU, price, cost, stock, and active status.
